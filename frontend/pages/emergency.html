When to Seek Emergency Care
  • Difficulty breathing, or bluish color around lips or face
  • High fever in a baby under 3 months old (38°C / 100.4°F or higher)
  • Unresponsive, unusually limp, or very difficult to wake
  • Seizures or convulsions
  • Persistent vomiting or signs of dehydration (no wet diapers for 6+ hours)
  • A fall or injury with loss of consciousness
  • Any situation that feels like a true emergency — trust your instincts

This list is for general awareness only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. When in doubt, contact your pediatrician or go to the nearest emergency room.
